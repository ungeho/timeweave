# Getting started

TimeWeave is a calendar for keeping your own schedule while telling other
people **only when you are free**. You can share "this time is taken" and "you
can reach me then" without ever showing them the schedule itself.

## What TimeWeave does

| It can | How |
| --- | --- |
| Keep your schedule | Timed events, all-day events and repeating events |
| Share without showing it | A share link lets someone check how busy you are without seeing any title or content |
| Point at reachable time | Mark an event as time you can be reached, and that is all the other person is told |

Whoever receives a share link can open it without signing in. In return, all
they see is **time ranges**. An event's title, notes and category never reach
them.

## Two settings to check first

Open **Settings** and check these two. On a computer it is at the top right of
the screen; on a phone it is inside the menu (☰) at the top right.

![The settings dialog. At the top the signed-in account, demo@example.test, then the time-zone field holding Asia/Tokyo, the note about all-day events, the display-language control, the note that language does not affect the shared calculation, and save and close buttons](/content/images/en/gs-settings.png)

The top line of Settings shows the account you are signed in as (its email
address).

### Time zone

This setting **decides which day an all-day event belongs to**. While it is
empty, a period that holds both an all-day event and timed time you can be
reached cannot have its open time shown on a share link. It is hidden to stay
safe.

Choose the time zone you live in. The settings dialog says the same thing.

Changing it changes how all-day events are read.
[Visibility and open time](/en/docs/privacy-and-availability) explains this.

### Display language

The interface is available in Japanese and English. **Follow browser setting**
follows whatever your browser asks for.

This setting only changes what the screen says. It **does not affect how
shared open time is calculated**.

## The three calendar views

On a computer (a screen 720px wide or more), switch between them with the
buttons at the top. The phone layout is described next, under **The calendar
on a phone**.

| View | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| Month | Seeing how full a month is at a glance |
| Week | Checking what is where in the day. Weeks start on Monday |
| Day | Reading a single day hour by hour |

**Today** returns to the period containing today. The arrows move back and
forward.

![The month view. A toolbar on top holds today, the arrows, the month/week/day switch and share; weeks start on Monday. Events sit in each day cell, and an all-day event spans several days as one bar](/content/images/en/gs-month-view.png)

In the month view weeks start on **Monday**. An all-day event appears as one
bar spanning the days it covers.

![The week view. An all-day lane runs across the top, with a 24-hour time scale down the side. Timed events sit in the day columns, and the one marked as open time is drawn with hatching](/content/images/en/gs-week-view.png)

In the week view all-day events sit together in a lane across the top, and
timed events sit on a 24-hour scale below it. **A hatched event is one marked
as Available.**

## The calendar on a phone

On a narrow screen such as a phone (less than 720px wide), the calendar has
two views, **Agenda** and **Month**. It starts in **Agenda**.

![The top of the calendar on a phone. Next to TimeWeave are the theme and menu buttons; below them Share, the date with previous and next arrows, the Today, Agenda and Month switches, a strip of seven days from Monday to Sunday, the selected day’s event and an add-on-this-day button](/content/images/en/gs-mobile-agenda.png)

| Part | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| The seven days | One week, Monday to Sunday. Press a day to list its events below. A day with events has a small dot |
| ‹ › | Move back or forward a week in **Agenda**, or a month in **Month** |
| Today | Return to today |
| Agenda | Lists the chosen day's events, timed ones and all-day ones apart |
| Add an event on this day | Creates a new event on the chosen day |

![The month view on a phone. A month of dates starting on Monday, with a small dot under each day that has events and the selected 19th highlighted](/content/images/en/gs-mobile-month.png)

**Month** shows a whole month of dates. It is a compact view that only marks
days with events with a dot; press a date to switch to that day's **Agenda**.

The computer's month, week and day views are not used on a phone.

## What is different on a phone

On a screen less than 720px wide, these parts differ from a computer. The
features are the same: anything you can do on a computer you can do on a
phone.

![The header on a phone. The menu button opens a menu with Settings, User guide and Sign out; the theme button stays in the header](/content/images/en/gs-mobile-menu.png)

| Part | Computer | Phone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Settings, User guide, Sign out | Side by side at the top right | Inside the menu (☰) at the top right |
| Switching the theme | Top right | Top right (outside the menu) |
| Creating and editing events | A dialog in the middle of the screen | A full-screen editor |
| Sharing | A dialog opened from **Share** above the calendar | A full-screen view opened from **Share** under the header |
| Entering dates and times | Typing, with the calendar and clock as helpers | Tap a field and pick from a calendar or clock |

When you are not signed in there is no menu, and a link to **User guide** is
shown directly.

This guide opens from **User guide** in the app (inside the menu on a phone).
**Back to the calendar** at the top of every guide page returns you to the app.

## Creating an event

1. On a computer, pick an empty day or time on the calendar; on a phone,
   press **Add an event on this day** in **Agenda**. The event dialog opens
2. Enter a **Title**
3. Set the date and time. Type the date and time, or pick them from the
   calendar and clock. For something lasting the whole day, tick **All day**
4. Under **Visibility**, choose how much a share link may disclose
5. Under **Availability**, choose whether you can be reached at that time
6. Press **Save**

Each field is explained in [Events](/en/docs/managing-events).
